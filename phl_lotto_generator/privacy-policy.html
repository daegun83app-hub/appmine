Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 20, 2026

ADMINE ("we", "us", or "our") provides the mobile application LOTTO PICK (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, used, stored, shared, and protected when you use the App.

1. Information We Process

The App may process the following information to provide and improve the service.

A. Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information, such as operating system, device model, app version, language, screen size, and crash or diagnostic signals
  • Advertising identifiers and advertising-related data used by Google AdMob where permitted by applicable law, platform settings, and Google policies
  • App usage events, such as screen views, button taps, ad impressions, ad clicks, and feature usage events through Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging token and notification delivery data when notifications are enabled
  • Network information, such as IP address, when connecting to app services, advertising services, analytics, or notification services

B. Information stored on your device

  • Lotto number sets, labels, and related notes saved by you
  • App settings, including language, notification prompts, promotional access status, and review prompt counters
  • Temporary app data needed for number generation, simulations, and local app operation

This local information is stored on your device, for example through SharedPreferences, and is not automatically uploaded to our server.

C. Information you provide directly

The App does not require account registration and does not directly collect your name, phone number, or email address inside the App. If you contact us for support, we use the contact details and message you provide only to respond to your request.

2. How We Use Information

  1. To provide number generation, saved numbers, dream number search, simulations, and result-related features
  2. To display and measure ads, including non-personalized or personalized ads where permitted
  3. To analyze usage statistics and improve App quality, performance, and usability
  4. To send push notifications only when you allow notifications
  5. To maintain security, prevent abuse, diagnose errors, and operate app services
  6. To respond to support inquiries

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that may process information according to their own privacy policies.

4. App Permissions

  • Internet — to load draw data, dream keyword data, ads, analytics, and notifications
  • Notifications — to send push notifications when you opt in
  • Advertising ID — to support ads and ad measurement, where available and permitted
  • Sensors — to support entertainment effects, such as lotto draw animations

You may change or revoke some permissions, such as notifications, from your device settings.

5. Retention

  • Local saved data remains on your device until you delete it, clear App data, or uninstall the App.
  • Analytics, advertising, and notification data are retained according to the policies of the relevant third-party services.
  • Support inquiry records are retained only as long as necessary to respond to the inquiry and meet legal or operational requirements.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, object to, or restrict certain processing of your personal information. You may also:

  • Limit ad personalization through your device settings or Google ad settings
  • Disable push notifications through your device notification settings
  • Delete locally saved data by clearing App data or uninstalling the App
  • Contact us to request privacy-related support

7. Children and Responsible Use

The App is not directed to children. Because the App relates to lottery-style entertainment, it is intended for users who are legally permitted to access lottery-related content in their location. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. International Processing

Information may be processed in countries outside your country of residence, including Korea, the United States, or other countries where our service providers operate. We use reasonable measures to protect information in connection with such processing.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, including limited access, secure communications where available, and data minimization. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, law, or our data practices change. We will post the updated version in the App or on this page. Continued use of the App after an update may be treated as acceptance of the updated policy where permitted by law.
